# Irish property search links: feasibility and published conditions

Checked 4 October 2026. Research only; no application source changed, no listing inventory collected or saved, no private API inspected, and no account required. Public browser controls and first-party policy pages were used. Findings are recorded in the resolved [property-search link feasibility](../../.scratch/ireland-service-map/issues/18-property-search-links.md) ticket; [the area handoff contract](../../.scratch/ireland-service-map/issues/19-property-search-area-contract.md) remains open for human decisions.

## Finding

The requested handoff is technically feasible: ordinary outbound URLs can carry a provider area, buy/rent, minimum bedrooms and maximum euro price. **Daft.ie is the recommended initial destination; Property.ie is a working alternative under the same Daft terms umbrella. MyHome technically preserves filters, but its published linking terms restrict links to its homepage.** Do not publish MyHome filtered links unless an applicable exception/permission has been established. This is a factual reading of published conditions, not legal approval of the product. [Daft terms, sections 1 and 31](https://support.daft.ie/hc/en-ie/articles/5127313728273-Terms-and-Conditions-of-use-of-Daft), [Property.ie terms, sections 1 and 31](https://www.property.ie/terms-of-service/), [MyHome terms, section 11](https://news.myhome.ie/uncategorized/terms-and-conditions-36589).

## Filter mapping and limitations

- **Daft:** buy route `/property-for-sale/{provider-area-slug}` with `salePrice_to` and `numBeds_from`; rent route `/property-for-rent/{provider-area-slug}` with `rentalPrice_to` and `numBeds_from`. Do not substitute the guessed generic key `price_to`. The UI exposed minimum bedrooms 0–15 and discrete price options. Sales €500k and rentals €2,500 are verified; arbitrary unlisted budget values were not tested. [Verified buy example](https://www.daft.ie/property-for-sale/dublin-city?salePrice_to=500000&numBeds_from=3), [verified rent example](https://www.daft.ie/property-for-rent/dundrum-dublin?rentalPrice_to=2500&numBeds_from=2).
- **Property.ie:** buy `/property-for-sale/{county}/{optional-area}/price_0-{max}/beds_{min}/`; rent uses `/property-to-let/`. `0` represented “No Min”; `beds_2` visibly represented `2+ Beds`, rather than exactly two. Minimum bedroom options were 1+ through 5+; a request for 6+ cannot be claimed supported by this observed UI. Rental prices offered €50 steps through €5,000 and then €10,000/€15,000/€20,000; sales offered discrete options. Arbitrary values were not tested. [Verified buy](https://www.property.ie/property-for-sale/dublin/price_0-500000/beds_3/), [verified rent](https://www.property.ie/property-to-let/dublin/dundrum/price_0-2500/beds_2/).
- **MyHome:** `maxprice` and `minbeds` persist in the observed buy and rent URLs. County/town path formats differ from Daft. This remains a technical finding subject to its explicit homepage-only linking condition. [Public buy search](https://www.myhome.ie/residential/dublin/property-for-sale?maxprice=400000&minbeds=3), [terms](https://news.myhome.ie/uncategorized/terms-and-conditions-36589).
- **Geography requires an explicit mapping.** The Daft search field showed separate suggestions for Dundrum in Dublin, Tipperary and Down. Property.ie scoped Dundrum through County Dublin. Provider names/slugs and their coverage are not CSO urban geometries. Keep a reviewed mapping from our `placeId` to provider area routes; do not derive arbitrary slugs and assume matching boundaries. County, town/area and Dublin City examples are verified, not every Ireland location. Multi-area URLs, province/region routes, exact polygons and arbitrary map coordinates remain unverified. If no exact mapping exists, offer a clearly labelled county fallback or the provider's search page; do not imply exact selected-area coverage.
- **Budget units:** pass `budgets.buy` as total euros and `budgets.rent` as monthly euros for the observed residential searches. Do not annualize rent. Supported discrete choices may require a disclosed approximation; never silently round a maximum upward. Missing budgets should omit the budget criterion, not become a zero maximum. This is a proposed adapter rule; omission behavior for every provider/filter combination was not tested.
- **Service criteria stay local.** Hospitals, schools, transport access, household ages and local scoring are not represented by the observed property URL filters. A link carries the housing criteria and selected provider geography only. Provider results may include promoted development cards or broader provider-defined coverage; this work did not certify every result against every criterion.

## Published conditions

**Daft:** section 31 conditionally permits linking to the Website, provided it is fair/legal, does not damage or exploit its reputation, does not suggest association/approval/sponsorship/endorsement without permission, is from a website the linker owns, and is not established to directly compete with the Website and redirect traffic from it. Permission can be withdrawn; framing is prohibited. Sections 8 and 27 restrict automated retrieval, database population, scraping, caching for others and incorporating site content. These observations support an ordinary user-clicked outbound link approach, but do not determine whether this particular product meets every condition. [Daft terms](https://support.daft.ie/hc/en-ie/articles/5127313728273-Terms-and-Conditions-of-use-of-Daft).

**Property.ie:** its own section 31 publishes the same conditional linking and no-framing approach. Section 1 identifies Daft Media terms covering Daft, Rent, Property and Let domains; this is an alternate interface, not an independent policy/provider option. Its restrictions also exclude automated retrieval/database population. [Property.ie terms](https://www.property.ie/terms-of-service/).

**MyHome:** section 11 allows homepage linking subject to fair/legal use, ownership of the linking website and no implied endorsement, then expressly restricts links to other parts of the site and prohibits framing. Filtered-search routes therefore conflict with its published linking condition unless an applicable exception or permission is established. Technical success does not remove that condition. [MyHome terms](https://news.myhome.ie/uncategorized/terms-and-conditions-36589).

The user's proposed avoidance of listing ingestion is appropriate for this scope. The relevant link/collection conditions live in terms of use; a privacy policy alone does not establish linking permission.

## Proposed next decision

Use Daft as the initial filtered destination and optionally Property.ie as an alternate interface, subject to the published conditional linking rules. Keep MyHome as a homepage-only option pending provider permission. Later implementation should build a pure local URL adapter, use explicit provider-area mappings and show the destination plus criteria before a user clicks. Open the provider in a normal top-level tab, with no fetch, preview scraper, iframe, copied listing cards or inventory count. Provider URLs are observed public behavior, not a versioned integration contract; recheck a small representative buy/rent sample before release and when the provider UI changes.
