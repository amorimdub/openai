# Licensed local quality-of-life, utilities and housing adapters

Verified against original saved payloads on 4 October 2026. The adapter module implements 21 source/scope plans and does not fetch, modify or overwrite originals. Dataset-specific licence evidence URLs are copied from the preserved collection manifests. All plans use CC BY4.0 except South Dublin multi-use community centres (CC0) and the housing construction report (CC BY-SA4.0).

Before the database topology checks, adapters produced150,097 valid-schema records,41 source-defect quarantines,3,184 excluded logical rows and4,083 unlocated records, with zero duplicate IDs. PostgreSQL/PostGIS then detected11 invalid polygon geometries: eight DCC2016 polygons, one SDCC park, one GSI public protection polygon and one GSI group contribution polygon. Proposed frozen quarantine rules bind each decision to source/scope, original asset SHA256, raw locator, source identifier and canonical geometry SHA256. No `ST_MakeValid` repair is applied. The frozen rules were merged and four affected scopes regenerated: the final accepted total is150,086 with52 quarantined. All1,265 accepted local polygon records then passed PostGIS topology validation.

`local-adapter-data-check.json` records the full raw-payload adapter counts. `local-topology-check.json` records original adapter polygon checks, reasons, original asset hashes and canonical geometry hashes. `local-canonical-topology-check.json` independently records the original canonical-file polygon checks; `local-canonical-topology-after-quarantine.json` confirms all1,265 accepted polygon records are valid. Both record files and SHA256 digests. `raw-post-expansion-integrity.json` independently verifies that all518 original files (1,119,838,617 bytes) and the acquisition inventory remain unchanged, with no missing or additional raw files. Publication remains the root runner's responsibility; adapter acceptance alone does not claim database publication.

GeoJSON coordinates retain their geometry types, remove unneeded altitude ordinates and convert advertised CRS to WGS84. ArcGIS rings use the shared containment-based polygon conversion. Cork's saved GeoPackage is copied to an ephemeral file, opened read-only with Bun SQLite, and its known POINT geometries decoded; the original database is never edited. SharedStrings/cell values in the Uisce workbook are parsed with SheetJS, and formulas are not evaluated. CSV records retain logical row indexes, including quoted commas and escaped quotes.

The common records omit phone/email contact fields. DLR libraries, cultural facilities and heritage sites remain distinct categories rather than being mislabeled community centres. Streetlights remain infrastructure records with unknown operating status; water supply directories and housing reports remain nonspatial observations. A nearby lamp or supply point cannot prove a particular house's utility connection.

Fixture validation: `bun test tests/local-adapters.test.ts` passed9 tests and107 assertions; TypeScript typecheck passed. Tests cover mixed facility categories/contact omission, unlocated and dated lighting, corrupt-coordinate quarantine, GSI study dates and semantic boundaries, Irish Grid multipoints, CSV quoting/housing stages, XLSX supply directories, read-only GeoPackage decoding, and dataset-specific reuse metadata.

ESB capacity data, EPA SAFER monitoring and unlicensed NFGWS/household utility datasets remain outside these public adapters because reuse was not established. No verified open live housing availability or household water/electricity connection register has been substituted for those missing facts.
