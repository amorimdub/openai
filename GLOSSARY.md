# Ireland household location fit

Places, household needs, service access and housing-market evidence used to assess locations in Ireland.

## Language

**Town or city**:
The place a household selects as its starting location for exploration.

**Household criteria**:
The housing needs, education needs, healthcare-access needs, transport preferences, utility requirements and amenities a household wants considered.

**Vertical**:
One of the five assessment domains: health, transportation, quality of life, education or utilities.

**Recorded service**:
A facility or amenity represented in a source dataset. Being recorded does not establish suitability, availability or a household’s eligibility to use it.

**Area score**:
A calculated assessment of how a location matches a household’s selected criteria.
_Avoid_: Universal quality-of-life ranking

**Market context**:
Historical sale-price or rent evidence for a geographic area. It is distinct from the price and availability of a particular home.

**External home search**:
A search for advertised homes on a property website using the household's chosen location and supported housing filters. The household views the homes on that website.

**Property search area**:
A location or region recognised by a property website for searching its advertised homes. It may differ from the statistical boundary used to assess a town or city.

**Government housing project**:
A publicly supported housing development with a stated delivery stage. It is distinct from a currently available home and from a state-owned land parcel.

**Government housing scheme participation**:
A household’s involvement in a government housing programme. It is distinct from the location or delivery status of a housing project.

**Data coverage**:
The services and places represented by a source. Unknown coverage is distinct from verified absence of a service.

**Utility coverage**:
An evidenced network or service area. It is distinct from a confirmed connection or service at an individual property.

**Recorded location**:
The coordinates or boundary a source assigns to a facility, place or asset. It is distinct from a verified entrance or the position of a household.

**Statistical geography**:
A publisher-defined area used to report observations. Its code and boundary period distinguish it from other areas with similar names.

**Market observation**:
A published price or rent measure for a defined geography, property category and period. It is distinct from an individual sale or an available home.

**Source snapshot**:
A dated capture of a source's published information. It records what was available at retrieval and does not establish when every fact was observed.
