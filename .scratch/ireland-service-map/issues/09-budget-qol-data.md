# Which sources can support regional housing budgets and expanded quality-of-life layers?

## Question

Which official Irish open data support regional house/apartment sale-price summaries and possibly rents? Verify Property Price Register, CSO price/median tables, RTB rent data where relevant; schemas, download/API, reuse/license, geography, freshness, sampling, aggregation risks, house-versus-apartment distinctions, VAT/market-price handling and bedroom limitations. Find open sources for clubs, activities, shops and green areas beyond existing parks/community findings; separate static venue records from current events and accessible services. Recommend an honest budget indication and quality-of-life baseline, not live property availability.


## Answer

Official CSO APIs support house/apartment monthly regional sale medians (HPM05), rolling all-dwelling medians (HPM07/HPM08), and RTB rents by location/type/bedroom (RIQ02). PPR bulk schema was parsed and cannot distinguish house/apartment or bedrooms by its structured description; no verified live inventory source. Sport Ireland national clubs/activity feeds are usable; OSM can provide shops/green-space baseline. Retain period, geographic level, licence, sample/coverage and unknown states.
