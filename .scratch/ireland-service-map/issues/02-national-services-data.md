## Question

Which official Irish sources offer reusable schools, hospital/health facility and registered early-years service locations? Verify exact dataset/resource URLs, formats, coordinates, licenses/access gates, freshness, Dublin filtering and sample usability. Separate location from capacity, availability and quality. Recommend importable hackathon sources with unresolved gates stated.

## Answer

Resolved through [national services findings](../../../docs/research/ireland-national-services.md). Pobal supplies licensed national childcare points; GeoHive/HSE supplies licensed but 2020 hospital points. Current Department school points work and distinguish primary/post-primary/special, but their exact reuse license remains unresolved. An explicitly licensed national 2014/15 school layer was sampled as a historical demonstration fallback. Tusla points/register add regulatory identifiers and possible age/service enrichment, with license/population/join gates. Republic of Ireland coverage is verified; Northern Ireland is not. Counts and GeoJSON samples were tested; full snapshot/import completeness and current suitability/availability were not.
