## Question

Which official reusable Republic-wide datasets or services describe group/community water schemes, public mains water, electricity supply, public street lighting and broadband? User clarified ESB electricity and public street lighting, both; investigate them separately. Verify exact resources, formats, reuse licenses, access gates, geographic/property granularity, freshness, and real sample usability. Distinguish area/network availability from an individual home's connection, speed, reliability or eligibility. Recommend practical hackathon information layers and explicit unsupported claims.


## Resolution comment

Resolved 4 October 2026. [Ireland utility information layers: verified sources and limits](../../../docs/research/ireland-utilities.md) records exact resources, licenses, real schemas and freshness. Recommend CC BY4 ComReg public-view small-area broadband coverage; Uisce water directory (692 zones, no geometry), EPA RAL regulatory supply points and GSI source-protection context; council streetlight subsets. ESB national capacity workbook is technically usable but public redistribution lacks an open licence; link only pending permission. NFGWS map/SAFER monitoring workbook have separate reuse gates. None establishes household connection, fibre speed or current lamp operation. No implementation, branches or commits; map unchanged.
