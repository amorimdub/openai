## Question

Which primary open datasets provide community centres, parks and government housing projects across Ireland, with Dublin council sources as verified local examples? Verify source and resource URLs, formats, coordinates, license, coverage, publisher dates versus observation dates, and sample resource usability. Distinguish planned housing, delivered housing, land banks, and availability. Identify national coverage gaps, ingestion risks and recommended hackathon sources.

## Resolution comment

Resolved 4 October 2026. [Ireland parks, community facilities and public housing source investigation](../../../docs/research/dublin-local-services.md) contains verified national/council URLs, resource schemas, dates, licensing and observed defects. Recommend an OSM national parks/community baseline with official council enrichments and explicit coverage flags. National Q1 2026 social housing report supplies 3,208 schemes across 31 Republic authorities but no coordinates: use information lists plus vetted LDA project pins. Keep state land separate. Government housing is informational. Fingal centres sample has reversed coordinates; SDCC housing sources are non-spatial tables. Northern Ireland official coverage and nationwide council completeness remain gaps. No research branch/commit: repository has no initial commit.
