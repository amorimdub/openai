## Question

For Republic-wide planning, which reusable official sources identify current GPs, hospital service capabilities, airports, public transport and cycling infrastructure? What routing data/engine evidence is needed to assess user criteria of hospital access within 30 minutes and GP care within a one-hour drive? Verify primary endpoints/resources, reuse terms, actual source age, schema/sample usability and mode/time assumptions. Distinguish current service suitability and accepting patients from mapped presence. Link existing transport research, research only the expanded requirements, and state when a time requirement cannot be evaluated.

## Answer

See [health, transport and journey-time findings](../../../docs/research/ireland-health-transport-times.md). Verified licensed historical GP data (including swapped coordinate attributes), current HSE website/bulk-rights gaps, official airport references and public-domain fallback, regional protected-cycle GeoJSON, routing engine requirements and explicit unknown states for unproven time criteria.
